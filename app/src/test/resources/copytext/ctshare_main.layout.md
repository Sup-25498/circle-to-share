# Circle to Share

Grab the screen from anywhere, crop it, and share it. Nothing is saved to your device → nothing to clean up.

## = Setup Assistant ·Fallback

How to open it Floating button off

# Crop tool

Gestures:

- Double-tap inside the selection: share
- Long-press inside the selection: copy
- Double-tap with nothing selected: close

# Circle to crop O

Draw a freeform loop around what you want and its outline becomes the crop box. Turn off to drag a rectangle directly.
